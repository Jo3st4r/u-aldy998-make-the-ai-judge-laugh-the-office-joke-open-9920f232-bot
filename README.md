## The joke

> Our printer has been 'warming up' since Monday, and honestly it has more job security than any of us.

1 printer, 4 days warming up, 0 jobs printed: the joke opens on a misery every office shares — a printer that never prints, only warms up — and turns it on the people living with it: the one thing that never works is the one thing nobody can fire. The laugh lands because it is true in every office, and the punch is a comparison, not a complaint.
